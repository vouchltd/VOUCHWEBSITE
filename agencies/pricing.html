Pricing

Priced per location. You keep the margin.

You decide what to charge your own clients. We charge you by the location.

Per location

$25 per location per month

5 location minimum, so at least $125 a month.

  • Reply drafts in a voice profile for each location
  • Approval queue for your team
  • Dashboard for every location in one place
  • Weekly report you can forward to your client
  • Optional auto-post for positive reviews

A location is one Google Business Profile.

How agencies can think about it

At 10 locations the cost to you is $25 x 10 = $250 a month. What you charge each client is your call, so the difference is yours. We do not set your resale price.
