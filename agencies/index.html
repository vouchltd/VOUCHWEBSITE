How it works

Three steps. Your team stays in charge.

01

Connect your clients' profiles

Link each Google Business Profile through Google's own permission screen. We never ask for a password.

02

Set a voice for each location

Tone, selling points, and things never to say. Every location gets its own profile.

03

Approve, then post

New reviews get a draft. Your team reads it, edits it if needed, and approves it. Then it posts.

See the full walkthrough

The approval queue

One queue for every location.

Drafts wait here. Positive reviews can post on their own if you allow it. Negative reviews always wait for a person.

Safety

A bad auto-reply should never cost you a client.

Negative reviews always wait for a human to approve. No setting turns that off.

  • Auto-post is optional, set per location, and limited to positive reviews.
  • Each location has its own "never say" list.
  • We never ask for passwords.
