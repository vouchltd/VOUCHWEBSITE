How it works

From new review to approved reply in five steps.

Your team makes the call at every point that matters. We do the first draft.

  1. 1

    Connect profiles

    Link each client's Google Business Profile with Google's own sign-in and permission screen. We never see or store a password. You can disconnect a location at any time.

  2. 2

    Set a voice per location

    Each location gets a voice profile. Write down the tone, the selling points worth mentioning, and the things the business never says. Two locations of the same client can sound different if they should.

  3. 3

    Replies draft

    When a new review comes in, our agent writes a reply using that location's voice profile and the review itself. Drafts land in one queue for all of your locations.

  4. 4

    Your team approves

    Someone on your team reads each draft, edits it if needed, and approves it. Negative reviews always stop here. They never post without a person approving them.

  5. 5

    Replies post

    Approved replies post to the client's Business Profile. If you allow it, positive reviews can skip the queue and post on their own. That is a per-location setting, and it is off until you turn it on.

Also included

  • A dashboard that shows every location in one place.
  • A weekly report you can forward to your client.

What you control

  • The voice profile for each location.
  • Whether positive reviews auto-post, location by location.
  • Who on your team approves replies.
