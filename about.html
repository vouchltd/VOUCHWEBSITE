About

Two students in Long Beach, building this on purpose.

We are two students. Vouch SoCal is a young company, and we would rather say that up front than hide it.

We started by selling Google Business Profile and review management to local business owners, one conversation at a time. That was slow, because every sale needed us in the room.

Agencies already manage profiles for many clients, and replying to reviews is some of the most repetitive work they do. So we moved to build a tool for them. It drafts the reply in each client's voice, your team approves it, and then it posts.

We have no client logos, case studies, or testimonials to show you yet. The free pilot is how we earn the first ones. If it does not save your team time, tell us and we will fix it or say so.
